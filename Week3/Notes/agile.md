
# Agile

## The Starting Point

### The Agile Manifesto

- Individuals and Interactions **over** Process and tools
- Working Software **over** Comprehensive Documentation
- Customer collaboration **over** Contract negotiation
- Responding to change **over** following a plan

## Agile Scrum

### The Players

- [The software development team.](#the-team)
- [The product owner](#product-owner)
  - Commonly starts with the product manager but can change as the team matures
- [The ScrumMaster]()
  - Partially agile coach/referee

### The Team

#### Composition

- Team Size: 5-9 people (typically)
  - Scales through teams of teams
- Team members should be dedicated
  - No participation in multiple teams
  - Should "sit together" in a shared space
- Self-organize in response to a challenge
- Are cross-functional

#### Responsibilities

- Deciding how much work to accept into a sprint
- Collectively committing to achieving that goal
- Working diligently to produce high-quality, "potentially shippable" functionality every sprint
- Demonstrate work to the product owner after each sprint
  - and within the sprint as appropriate
- Working at a sustainable pace

### Product Owner

#### Product Owner: Responsibilities

- Convey vision
- Define features
- Prioritize and maintains the product backlog
- Manages stakeholders
- Makes scope/schedule trade off
- Responsible for financial success
- Accepts or rejects work results

### ScrumMaster

#### ScrumMaster: Responsibilities

- Ensure Scrum is understood and enacted within the team and org
  - Enforce values and principles
- Helps the team do it's best work
  - Remove obstacles and impediments
  - Protects the team from disruptions and distractions
- Act as change agent
- Coaches team and organization
- Facilitates communication over meetings
- The "Process Owner" - Servant/Leader

### The Sprint

Sprint planning -> Daily Scrum -> Sprint Review -> Sprint Retrospective -> Sprint Planning -> ...

#### The Sprint: Planning

- Product owner brings prioritized backlog
- Product owner explains high-priority items to team
- Team pulls story out of backlog and into sprint
  - Decompose the story to tasks
  - Product owner answers questions
  - Team decides an ideal hour estimate for each task
  - team commits to completing the story
- Rinse and repeat

#### The Sprint: Daily Scrum

- Parameters
  - Daily
  - 15 minutes
- Purpose
  - **Not** for problem solving **but** for problem identification
  - Individual team member commitment
  - Identification of impediments

#### The Sprint: Review

- Parameters
  - max 4 hours
  - informal, no slides, minimal prep
- Purpose
  - Get feedback on delivered features
  - Adjust product backlog as appropriate
- Whole world is invited
- Whole team participates
